FAQ

Common questions


Which businesses do you work with?

Mainly local service businesses such as roofing, plumbing, HVAC, cleaning, waterproofing and waste removal that want more relevant calls and form leads. I also support dental and other service campaigns when it is a good fit.

Can you audit my existing Google Ads account?

Yes. I review campaign structure, keywords, search terms, targeting, ads, conversion actions and the landing page, then list the highest-priority issues.

Can you set up GTM, GA4 and Google Ads conversion tracking?

Yes. The exact setup depends on your website and lead journey. I configure the agreed events and test them with preview and debugging tools.
