My process

Find the problem before changing the campaign.

I avoid changing everything at once. I check the data, identify the likely issue, make focused improvements and measure what changes.

STEP 01

Audit

Review campaign settings, search terms, conversion actions and current tracking.

STEP 02

Find the issue

Identify wasted spend, weak intent, tracking gaps or landing-page friction.

STEP 03

Fix & optimize

Prioritize changes based on evidence and your lead-generation goals.

STEP 04

Test & report

Validate tracking, measure results and explain what to improve next.

Why tracking matters

Bad data makes good decisions harder.

Before increasing budgets or making major campaign changes, make sure your important lead actions are being measured correctly.

What I check during an audit

  • Are phone calls and form submissions tracked correctly?
  • Are conversion actions firing once and recording the right events?
  • Are search terms relevant to the services you actually offer?
  • Are location targeting and campaign settings aligned with your service area?
  • Does the landing page make it easy for a potential customer to contact you?
  • Can we connect campaign spend with the leads your business receives?
FAQ

Questions before we get started.

Not sure what your account needs? Start with a short conversation about your goals and current setup.

Which businesses do you work with?

My main focus is local service businesses such as roofing, plumbing, HVAC and cleaning companies that want more relevant calls and form leads.

Can you audit an existing Google Ads account?

Yes. I can review campaign structure, keywords and search terms, targeting, ads, conversion actions and landing-page experience, then outline the highest-priority issues I find.

Can you set up GTM, GA4 and Google Ads conversion tracking?

Yes. The exact setup depends on your website and lead journey. I can configure the agreed events and test them using available preview and debugging tools.

Do you guarantee a specific number of leads?

No. Lead volume and cost depend on competition, budget, service area, website experience and other factors. I focus on sound setup, measurable improvements and transparent reporting.

What do you need to start?

Usually, a short description of your business, service areas and goals is enough for an initial discussion. If an audit requires account access, we can agree on the minimum access needed.
