Everything you need to know

Not sure what your account needs? Start with a short call about your goals.

Which businesses do you work with?

Mainly local service businesses such as roofing, plumbing, HVAC and cleaning companies that want more relevant calls and form leads. I also support dental and other service campaigns when it's a good fit.

Can you audit my existing Google Ads account?

Yes. I review campaign structure, keywords, search terms, targeting, ads, conversion actions and the landing page, then list the highest-priority issues.

Can you set up GTM, GA4 and Google Ads conversion tracking?

Yes. The exact setup depends on your website and lead journey. I configure the agreed events and test them with preview and debugging tools.

Do you guarantee a number of leads?

No. Results depend on competition, budget, service area and your website. I focus on sound setup, measurable improvements and transparent reporting.

What do you need to get started?

A short description of your business, service areas and goals. If an audit needs account access, we agree on the minimum access required.

How much does it cost?

Work is scoped and quoted before it starts. Ad spend is paid directly to Google and is separate from my fee.
